``analytic_budget_number`` exposes the budget number of a purchase order
line as a field of its own, but Odoo has no menu over the purchase order
lines to show it in. The OCA module ``purchase_order_line_menu`` adds
one under *Purchase > Orders*.

This module brings the two together: the budget number becomes a column
of the list that menu opens, and a field and a group-by of its search
view. It is a glue module, and it is installed automatically once both
of the modules it depends on are.

The budget number is only shown to a user of the *Analytic Accounting*
group, as an analytic account is of no use to anyone else.
