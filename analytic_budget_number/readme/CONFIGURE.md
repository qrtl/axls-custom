Go to *Invoicing > Configuration > Analytic Accounting > Analytic Plans*, open
the analytic plan whose accounts stand for budget numbers, and enable *Use for
Budget Numbers*. Each company can have one such plan, and a plan shared by every
company excludes the plans of the individual ones.

The accounts of the subplans of that plan count as budget numbers as well, which
is how the analytic distribution itself resolves them.

The values of the attributes are master data, maintained from the same
*Analytic Accounting* section: *Budget Satellites*, *Budget Subsystems*,
*Budget Components* and *Budget Models*. Whoever may edit an analytic account
may maintain them. A value that falls out of use can be archived: it stays on
the accounts that carry it and drops out of the list the next account picks
from.
